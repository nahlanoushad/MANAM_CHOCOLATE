## 3. Product Template Technical Architecture

### A. Template File: `woocommerce/content-single-product.php`
- **Two-Column Responsive Layout:** Left media column with dynamic vertical thumbnails strip (desktop) and horizontal swipe strip (mobile); Right information column.
- **Header Elements Order:**
  1. Title (`.product-meta__title`, `HeroinePro-Regular`, 28px, color `#282828`).
  2. Veg Badge Icon (`.product-veg-icon`, green square with inner dot) + Net Weight & Unit Price.
  3. Price & Taxes (`.product-meta__price`, `FS Meridian Bold`, 20px, + `(Incl. of Taxes)`).
  4. Genuine Review Star Ratings & Count (rendered strictly when verified reviews exist > 0).
  5. Lifestyle & Dietary Highlights Chips (Deduplicated: Vegan, Nut Free, Celiac Friendly, etc.).
  6. Taste Descriptors Table (Tasting notes, Origin, Cacao percentage).
  7. Delivery Pincode Checker (6-digit Indian PIN input with black Check button).
  8. Purchasing Controls (Native Quantity selector with +/- buttons and full-width Add to Cart button).
- **5-Section Information Accordion / Tabs:**
  1. `DESCRIPTION`
  2. `PRODUCT INFO` (Shelf life, Country of Origin, Manufactured By, Net Weight)
  3. `INGREDIENTS`
  4. `STORAGE INFORMATION`
  5. `ALLERGEN INFORMATION`
- **Lower Recommendation Section:**
  - Subtitle `MORE FROM`
  - Dynamic `[COLLECTION / SERIES NAME]` heading
  - 4 Curated Related Product Cards with image hover secondary swap and Add-to-Cart.
